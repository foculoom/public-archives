Progress without a made-up score.

Known ≠ complete

Test passes describe specific checks. They do not establish usability, public availability or product demand. Different test types are never added into one success rate.

Unknown stays visible

Missing measurements are null in the data and “Unknown” here. Elapsed time needs evidenced start and end points; it never means active work hours.
